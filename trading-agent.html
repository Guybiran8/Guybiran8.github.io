Trading Agent

My automated stock scanning agent.

View the code

What I built

The agent scans stocks in the Nasdaq, Russell and S&P 500, looks at their charts, and analyzes breakouts using support and resistance, moving averages, volume and more. When it finds a good stock, it sends me a message on Telegram that tells me the price to exit at if it goes down or up and the potential gain. Then it tracks the stock until it exits. I came up with the strategy ideas and ways to fix problems, and Claude did most of the coding.

Challenges

At first I thought the agent was genuinely broken, like it had all these indicators just to guess what's a buy and what isn't. But it just couldn't track a trade the way it was supposed to. It could find a stock, but it couldn't take profit. I scanned the agent for all of its issues and realized it could only exit trades on a stop loss, so I made it also exit wins when the target hits.

What I learned

I was quick to assume the agent was broken instead of checking it myself. A bad number doesn't always mean a bad strategy. Sometimes the problem is in how it's measured, so now I check how results are counted before I judge something.

Results

Honestly, nothing yet. But it's the project that got me into coding and started everything, including Stock Debate. It's been running on my server since May and used to send me alerts on Telegram, but I turned them off because they were sending bad picks. I've fixed the exit bug, but I still need to fix how it picks stocks before it's worth turning back on.
