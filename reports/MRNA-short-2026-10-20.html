What is being tested
  • The drug is intismeran autogene (also called V940 or mRNA-4157), a custom mRNA cancer treatment made for each patient from up to 34 targets found in that person’s tumor. It is given with Merck’s Keytruda after surgery (adjuvant treatment, meaning treatment meant to lower the chance the cancer returns). The disease is completely removed stage IIB to IV skin melanoma, with no earlier body-wide cancer drugs. The trial enrolled 1,137 patients, split 2 to 1, so about two-thirds got the combination.
    How it could move the price: This is the program investors are pricing. A clear added benefit over Keytruda alone supports a higher price. A thin added benefit, or new safety problems, supports a lower price.
    When: Monday, October 26, 2026, and the next few trading days.
    Already in the price: On August 19, 2026, the same day the companies said this trial had succeeded, Moderna’s stock rose about 177%, from about $63 to about $174. By late September it was near $200, up about 222% from mid-August, and some analysts said that price already assumes success in several cancers and sales far above their own estimates, not just this melanoma trial. The exact size of the benefit has not been published yet.
