What is being tested
  • The drug is intismeran autogene (also called V940 or mRNA-4157), a custom mRNA cancer shot made for each patient’s tumor, given with Merck’s Keytruda after surgery. The Phase 3 trial, INTerpath-001, enrolled 1,137 people with high-risk stage IIB-IV melanoma that had been fully cut out. Patients were split 2-to-1 between the combination and Keytruda plus a placebo (a dummy shot).
    How it could move the price: This is the study the stock has been trading on. A clear win versus Keytruda alone supports a higher price. A result that looks only modest versus Keytruda alone pressures the price.
    When: 2026-10-26
    Already in the price: Investors already expected a clear win after the companies said in August that the trial had succeeded, and the stock had risen sharply on that news. When the full numbers came out on October 22, analysts said the result was solid but not better than the high hopes already built into the price, and the stock fell about 18 percent that day as people sold the news. (reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmace) (ft.com/content/9e59746f-9b63-4adb-b868-1) (pharmaphorum.com/news/esmo26-moderna-msd)

  • The main result measured was recurrence-free survival (time until the cancer comes back or the patient dies). After a median 24.6 months, the combination cut that risk by 42% versus Keytruda alone (hazard ratio 0.58, a risk comparison where 1.00 means equal risk; p<0.001, a statistical test that this gap was very unlikely to be chance). At two years, 82.2% on the combination were still free of recurrence, versus 70.0% on Keytruda alone.
    How it could move the price: A 42% risk cut is a positive trial result and can support the stock. It is smaller than the 49% cut in the earlier study, which is one reason traders sold the news.
    When: 2026-10-26
    Already in the price: Investors already expected about a 40% to 50% cut in that risk, so the 42% result was in line with what the stock price already reflected [was already counted in the price] and sat at the low end of hopes. The shares had already risen more than 500% in 2026 on that expectation, then fell about 18% by the close of October 22 (as much as about 25% during the day) when the details leaked, a sell-the-news drop that erased roughly $17 billion to $24 billion in company value.
